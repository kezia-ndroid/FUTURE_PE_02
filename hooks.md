# Hooks — GlowRoots Skincare UGC Ad Pack

Platform: Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts
Product: Natural Glow Face Serum (Niacinamide + Turmeric)

---

## Hook 1 — Pain Hook
> "I spent ₹8,000 on skincare and my skin got worse."

**Why it works:** Leads with a financial + emotional pain point. Stops scroll instantly.

---

## Hook 2 — Question Hook
> "Why is nobody talking about what's actually in your serum?"

**Why it works:** Creates immediate curiosity and positions the brand as an insider secret.

---

## Hook 3 — Relatable Pain Hook
> "Dark spots at 22. I thought this was just my life now."

**Why it works:** Hyper-specific to target audience. Feels like a confession, not an ad.

---

## Hook 4 — Shocking Stat Hook
> "80% of Indian skincare products contain parabens. Mine did too."

**Why it works:** Credibility + fear + self-relevance. Makes viewers question what they're using.

---

## Hook 5 — Curiosity Hook
> "One serum changed my skin in 3 weeks. I need to show you."

**Why it works:** Promise of a reveal. Low commitment to keep watching.

---

## Hook 6 — Bold Claim Hook
> "This ₹399 serum outperformed my ₹2,500 one. Not clickbait."

**Why it works:** Price contrast creates instant intrigue. "Not clickbait" builds trust.

---

## Hook 7 — Question Hook (Conversational)
> "Can turmeric and niacinamide actually fix pigmentation? I tested it."

**Why it works:** Educational framing with a personal test. Appeals to ingredient-aware audience.

---

| Hook # | Best Platform | Ad Length | Use Case |
